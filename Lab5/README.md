## Lab Overview

To finish off the semesters let’s use the knowledge we’ve developed to have some fun. Each team is asked to develop a project of their choosing to demonstrate robot technology to their peers. The top 3 projects, as voted on by peers, will receive extra credit here.

You have two major options here:

1. Go deeper on Rosbot capabilities. Is there some other cabability you'd like to add to the Rosbot? Go deeper on one of the topics we've barely discussed or add more functionality to the bot. Give it a new goal!
2. Develop and build a new mechatronic or robotic system.

## Lab Grading

The grading of each lab is based 50\% on the successful completion of the lab. For this lab, that 50\% breaks down into:

- 20% Effort and ambition – This ambition can be shown either through the programming challenge or mechanical challenge. You can choose whether to modify your existing robot car or design a new project entirely.
- 20% Functionality of the end product – does it work?
- 10% Project polish - Does the project work smoothly or look well constructed? Does it just barely work or work nicely?
